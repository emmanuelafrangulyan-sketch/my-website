Tiki Bay 🌴

The central and largest island, and the main entrance to the Tiki Islands.

Sail past the welcome dock, through the giant tiki gate and up the torch-lit path into the village. The waterfall never stops, the huts never lock, and the harbor master (a very serious pelican) checks every boat twice.

Giant Tiki GateTwo carved tikis guard the way in. Say aloha or they frown harder.
Waterfall VillageBamboo huts that glow warm and golden after dark.
Welcome DockWhere every adventure (and most snack runs) begins.

Coconut Cave 🥥

A peaceful beach island of coconut palms, hammocks and beach chairs.

Hammocks swing between the palms, beach chairs face the waves and there is a frankly suspicious amount of coconuts. The cave at the back is perfect for naps, secrets and hiding from responsibilities.

Hammock GroveStriped hammocks with a strict no-alarm-clock policy.
Beach Chair RowFront-row seats to the turquoise water.
The Napping CaveCool, quiet and lit by a single cozy lantern.

Goo Lagoon 💧

A secluded island with crystal-clear water, waterfalls and tropical flowers.

A hidden lagoon so clear you can count the fish (there are fourteen, and one of them is a duck). Twin waterfalls pour from the cliffs, the flowers bloom all year, and after dark the water glows.

Crystal LagoonSo clear you can see your toes. And the duck.
Twin WaterfallsNature’s shower, available 24/7.
Glowing WaterThe lagoon sparkles turquoise every night.

Pineapple Peak 🍍

A mountainous island with lush vegetation and the best viewpoint in the archipelago.

The tallest point in the islands, shaped (by pure coincidence, Emul insists) exactly like a pineapple. Follow the zigzag trail through the jungle to the summit deck and its very old, very loyal telescope.

Zigzag TrailLantern-lit at night, so nobody gets lost. Probably.
Summit ViewpointSee every island at once from the wooden deck.
The CrownLeafy, spiky and visible from Sunset Beach.

Sunset Beach 🌅

A small, romantic island with a wooden pier, cozy seating and an uninterrupted ocean view.
